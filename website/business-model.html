04 — Core Engine
Decision Pipeline

The intelligence layer: a deterministic pipeline that runs before any LLM touches the output. Every program is evidence-based, safety-triaged, and vault-informed.

Safety Triage

9-dimension risk assessment. Cardiac, ortho, ED, psychological, GI, sleep, medical, mobility, lifestyle.

Vault RAG

FAISS semantic search + graph expansion across 1,563 chunks. Wikilink, same-pillar, same-category edges.

Vault Signals

Extract pillar scores, nutrition/training/recovery guidance from RAG context before pillar selection.

Book Engine

06 — Target Market
Market Positioning

Intermediate-to-advanced lifters (1-3+ years) who've hit plateaus. People who've outgrown YouTube advice but can't afford $200/mo personal training.

👤

Primary Persona

25-35 year old male, 1-3 years lifting, knows basic programming but stuck. Wants evidence-based guidance without the influencer noise. Price-sensitive, Egyptian market primary.

👩

Secondary Persona

Female lifter, intermediate, interested in recomposition and carb cycling. Values the nutrition book and TDEE engine. Engages with Arabic content.

🏆

Power User

Competitive powerlifter or bodybuilder. Wants the full Omni Hub + Premium Coaching. Values ACWR monitoring, e1RM tracking, and deload automation.

09 — Roadmap
Growth Roadmap

From PMF validation to competitive domination across 9 phases.

Phase 1 — Days 1-5

Critical Fixes

Fix critical bugs, security audit, measurement framework setup. Prepare the system for real users.

Phase 2 — Days 6-30

Beta Validation

Recruit 3-5 beta users, run first check-in cycle, execute Sean Ellis test. Gate: Day 7 retention ≥30%.

Phase 3 — Days 31-60

Expand & Measure

Expand to 15 users, PMF assessment decision gate. Gate: Sean Ellis ≥40% "very disappointed".

Phase 4 — Month 3-4

Mobile Launch

Ship Expo mobile app with full onboarding, chat, tracker. App Store + Play Store submission.

Phase 5 — Month 4-6

Desktop Launch

Ship Electron desktop app with local LM Studio integration. Offline-first, full pipeline.

Phase 6 — Month 6-9

Coach Marketplace

Open platform to other coaches. 80/20 revenue share. Coach dashboard, client management, billing.

Phase 7 — Month 9-12

International Expansion

Full Arabic localization across all channels. French market entry. Additional payment methods.

Phase 8 — Month 12-18

Advanced Features

Biofeedback autoregulation wired. ACWR alerts. Social features, share cards, routine deep links.

Phase 9 — Month 18+

Platform Play

API for third-party integrations. Gym chain partnerships. Wearable device sync. Research partnerships.
