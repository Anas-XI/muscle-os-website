Find your optimal weekly sets per muscle group

What is Training Volume? Training volume — measured as the number of hard sets per muscle group per week — is the primary driver of muscle hypertrophy. Research shows each muscle group has a Minimum Effective Volume (MEV), a Maximum Adaptive Volume (MAV), and a Maximum Recoverable Volume (MRV). Getting these right prevents both under-training and overtraining. This concept is covered in depth in the Training & Programming Book (27 chapters, 200+ references).
