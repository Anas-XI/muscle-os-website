RPE Load Calculator

Autoregulated load suggestions · e1RM tracking · Safety layer · Weekly volume dashboard

What is RPE? RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) is an autoregulation tool that measures how hard a set feels on a 1–10 scale. Combined with the Reps in Reserve (RIR) model, RPE-based training lets you adjust load daily based on how you actually perform — not how you planned to perform weeks ago. It is the most practical way to apply autoregulation to your training. This concept is covered in depth in the Training & Programming Book (27 chapters, 200+ references).
