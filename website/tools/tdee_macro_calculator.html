What is TDEE? TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is the total number of calories your body burns each day — including your BMR (basal metabolic rate), NEAT (non-exercise activity thermogenesis), EAT (exercise activity thermogenesis), and TEF (thermic effect of food). Understanding your TDEE is the foundation of any nutrition plan, whether your goal is fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance. This concept is covered in depth in the Diet & Nutrition Book (28 chapters, 200+ references).
