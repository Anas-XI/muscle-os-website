Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Digital Books

Our digital books (including the Training Book, Nutrition Book, and related bundles) are non-refundable once the access code has been redeemed.

2. Tool Subscriptions

For tool subscriptions (Training Tool, TDEE Engine, OMNI HUB), a refund is available within 7 days of code redemption, provided the tool has not been used (i.e., no saved sessions). After 7 days or after first use, these subscriptions are non-refundable.

3. Free Trial

The 7-day trial is completely free. No payment is collected, and therefore no refund is applicable.

4. Bundles

Bundled purchases are treated as a single purchase. Partial refunds are not available for individual items within a bundle.

5. How to Request

To request a refund, please contact us via WhatsApp. Provide your Google account email and the access code in your message. Eligible refunds will be processed within 5 business days to your original payment method.

6. Exceptions

If an access code was purchased but never redeemed (unused), a full refund is available regardless of the time elapsed since purchase.
