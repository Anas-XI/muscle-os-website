Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the Training App and Omni Hub?

The Training App handles your lifting (splits, volume, RPE logging). Omni Hub includes both the Training App AND the TDEE Adaptive Engine, linking them together so your nutrition automatically recalibrates based on your training fatigue.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! You can start a 7-day free trial of the tools directly from the links above. No credit card required upfront.

Can I pay internationally?

Yes. If you're outside Egypt, select the USD pricing. You can pay via international options or message Anas directly on WhatsApp for manual processing.

How do I get my access code after purchase?

If you pay online via card, your access code will be displayed instantly on the screen and emailed to you. If you pay manually via Vodafone Cash, it will be sent to your WhatsApp shortly after verification.

Do you offer refunds?

Because the tools offer a 7-day free trial, and digital books are immediately downloaded, we do not offer refunds after purchase.

Is it an app I download from the App Store?

It is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You access it via any browser and can "Add to Home Screen" on iOS or Android, which makes it look and act exactly like a native app (works offline!).

What's the AI Coach powered by?

Our Omni Hub AI Coach is powered by the Groq Llama 3.3 70B model, fine-tuned specifically on the Muscle OS 277-document knowledge vault. It gives evidence-based answers, not generic AI hallucinations.
