Yes. Every program starts from your intake answers, biomechanics assessment, equipment access, and training history — then gets adjusted weekly against your actual performance data. No template with your name pasted on it.

Absolutely. Volume targets, exercise selection, and progression all scale to your training age. Most clients start as beginners and most of the science used comes from evidence-based ranges that apply from day one.
