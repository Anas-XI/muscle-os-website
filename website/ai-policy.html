AI Safety, Ethics & Transparency Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 · Compliant with EU AI Act & FTC Transparency Guidelines
Critical Medical & Health Disclaimer Muscle OS AI systems, algorithmic engines, and automated coaching tools do NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical healthcare. They are intended solely for educational, recreational, and informational sports performance purposes for healthy adults. Always obtain medical clearance from a licensed physician before beginning any resistance training or nutritional alteration.

1. Scope & Purpose of AI in Muscle OS

Muscle OS utilizes artificial intelligence, heuristic algorithms, and vector retrieval (RAG) to provide evidence-based training and nutritional education. This policy outlines how AI operates within our software, the boundaries we enforce, and how athlete safety is prioritized.

1. Deterministic Mathematics

Caloric baselines, macro ratios, and RPE/RIR percentages are calculated using peer-reviewed scientific formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor, Katch-McArdle, Helms landmarks). These calculations are deterministic, repeatable, and rule-bounded.

2. Vector Search (RAG)

Research queries retrieve exact passages from Coach Anas's 277-document scientific research vault via FAISS vector embeddings, grounding all advice in published exercise science.

3. Large Language Models

LLM APIs (such as Google Gemini and Groq) synthesize retrieved literature and assist Coach Anas in drafting conversational explanations and workout adjustments.

4. Human Coach Oversight

Coach Anas Mo'men supervises the algorithmic coaching pipeline, monitors check-in anomalies, and handles client escalations.

2. Non-Medical & Non-Clinical Boundaries

The Muscle OS AI system is strictly constrained by the following non-negotiable boundaries:

  • No Clinical Diagnoses: The system will never diagnose eating disorders, orthopedic pathology, endocrine dysfunctions, or cardiovascular conditions. If red-flag symptoms are detected, the system immediately advises professional medical consultation.
  • No Prescription of Extreme Regimens: Algorithms enforce hard caloric floors (never prescribing diets below estimated basal metabolic rates without physician supervision) and volume ceilings (capping set volumes to prevent overtraining or rhabdomyolysis).
  • No Biometric Facial Scanning: Muscle OS strictly forbids and avoids biometric facial recognition or FaceID analysis on physique check-in photos.

3. Human-in-the-Loop Supervision

In accordance with high-standard AI governance principles, autonomous decision engines do not operate unchecked. Coach Anas Mo'men actively monitors intake submissions, high-risk triage flags (including SCOFF screening flags and rapid weight fluctuations), and coaching interactions. Users may request human review of any automated recommendation at any time via WhatsApp.

4. Data Privacy & Zero Public Model Training

We respect the confidentiality of your health, physical, and personal data. Muscle OS maintains an absolute guarantee:

  • Your personal workout logs, biometrics, weight data, and chat history are NEVER used to train or fine-tune public foundation AI models (including OpenAI, Google, or Groq public datasets).
  • API communications with model providers are governed by enterprise zero-data-retention and non-training terms.
  • All client-side data stored in your browser is sandboxed and encrypted/obfuscated.

5. Algorithmic Transparency & Hallucination Warnings

Generative AI systems are capable of occasional errors, misinterpretations, or hallucinations. Athletes must adhere to the following safety rules:

  • Never attempt a working load, exercise variation, or dietary restriction that causes sharp joint pain, dizziness, or physiological distress, regardless of what an automated calculator suggests.
  • Individual biomechanics, injury histories, and daily recovery states always supersede algorithmic projections.
  • You possess full autonomy: you can override, adjust, or discard any AI recommendation within your client tools at will.

6. Contact & AI Safety Reporting

If you encounter an unexpected or potentially unsafe AI output, please report it immediately to Coach Anas Mo'men via email at ai-safety@muscleos.coach or direct WhatsApp at +20 104 079 6017.
