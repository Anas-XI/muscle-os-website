Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what data is collected on muscleos.coach (and its tools, books, and bundles), how it's used, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Contact & coaching data: when you message via WhatsApp or sign up for coaching, we keep the conversation and any details you share (name, training history, goals, health notes).
  • Tool data: data you enter in our apps (training logs, weight, calories, programs) is stored locally in your browser and on our servers only where needed to run the service.
  • Order data: when you purchase access, the order page processes your details through Paymob or Vodafone Cash. We do not store card details.

How we use it

  • To deliver coaching, programs, and access to the tools you purchased.
  • To adjust your programming based on your check-in and performance data.
  • To verify payments and grant access codes.
  • To improve the site and services (anonymized usage patterns).

What we don't do

  • We never sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't share your data with third parties except where required to operate (payment processors, hosting) or by law.

Third-party services

Paymob (payments), Vodafone Cash (payments), Google Fonts (typography), and our hosting provider may process limited data (e.g., IP address) as part of normal operation. Each is governed by its own privacy policy.

Data retention & your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Contact via WhatsApp and it will be handled. Deleting data may cancel access to purchased services.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Message on WhatsApp or email the coach directly.
