Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains how Coach Anas Mo'men ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use muscleos.coach and associated tools, programs, and services.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is Coach Anas Mo'men (muscleos.coach). You can contact us directly via WhatsApp at https://wa.me/201040796017.

2. What We Collect

  • Google account info: Name, email address, and profile picture collected via Google Sign-In.
  • Training data: Workout logs, RPE, volume, and exercise history, which are stored locally in your browser and optionally synced to the cloud.
  • Nutrition data: Calories, macros, weight logs, and TDEE calculations.
  • Health screening data: Injury history, cardiac conditions, SCOFF eating disorder screening, gut health, sleep, and alcohol habits, collected via Telegram bot intake.
  • Order & payment data: Name, WhatsApp number, email, and payment reference (processed securely via Paymob or Vodafone Cash).
  • Usage analytics: Page views, session ID, and feature usage collected via localStorage.
  • Device & browser info: IP address and user agent via our hosting and CDN provider (Cloudflare).

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contract performance: To deliver coaching services, training programs, and tool access you requested.
  • Legitimate interest: For analytics, service improvement, and maintaining security.
  • Consent: For processing health data (GDPR Art. 9), marketing communications, and tracking cookies/analytics.

4. Special Category Health Data (GDPR Art. 9)

We collect health-related data (including injury history, cardiac screening, and eating disorder screening) ONLY with your explicit consent. This data is used solely for the purpose of personalizing your training program and ensuring your safety. You can withdraw your consent at any time; however, this will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our platform, which may process your data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google OAuth: Authentication (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare Workers & KV: API, edge computing, and data storage (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase: Database and PostgreSQL infrastructure (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Paymob & Vodafone Cash: Payment processing (Paymob and Vodafone Privacy Policies)
  • Groq: AI/LLM processing for coaching (Groq Privacy Policy)
  • WhatsApp / Meta: Communication (Meta Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts: Typography (Google Privacy Policy)

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We do NOT use traditional HTTP cookies for tracking. Instead, we use local storage in your browser for essential functions and analytics, including:

  • mos_session_id: Anonymous analytics session identifier.
  • mos_google_session: Google sign-in session token (7-day expiry).
  • mos_subscription: Access code verification state.
  • cookiesAccepted: Your consent preference.

Training and nutrition tool data is also stored in localStorage. Analytics tracking only activates after you accept the cookie consent banner.

7. Data Retention

  • Session tokens: 7 days
  • Access codes: 30 days for tools, lifetime for books
  • Training/nutrition logs: Retained until you clear your browser data or request deletion
  • Cloud-synced data: Retained until you request account deletion
  • Coaching conversations (WhatsApp/Telegram): Retained until you request deletion
  • Order records: Retained for 5 years as required by Egyptian tax law
  • Analytics data: Retained for 12 months

8. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure / Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw Consent: Especially regarding the processing of health data.
  • Opt-out of sale (CCPA): We do NOT sell your personal data.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us via WhatsApp at https://wa.me/201040796017. We will respond to your request within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed outside of your country on Cloudflare (global edge network), Supabase (US), and Google (US). These transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses and service provider agreements to ensure your data remains secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when the latest changes were made. Your continued use of the service after changes have been made constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us via WhatsApp at https://wa.me/201040796017 or on Instagram @anas_moamen1.
