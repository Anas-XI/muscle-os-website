Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Compliant with GDPR, BIPA, CAN-SPAM & Egyptian Law 151/2020

This privacy policy explains how Coach Anas Mo'men ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use muscleos.coach and associated tools, programs, and services.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is Coach Anas Mo'men (muscleos.coach). You can contact us directly via WhatsApp at https://wa.me/201040796017 or email at privacy@muscleos.coach.

2. What We Collect

  • Google account info: Name, email address, and profile picture collected via Google Sign-In.
  • Training data: Workout logs, RPE, volume, and exercise history, which are stored locally in your browser and optionally synced to the cloud.
  • Nutrition data: Calories, macros, weight logs, and TDEE calculations.
  • Health screening data: Injury history, cardiac conditions, SCOFF eating disorder screening, gut health, sleep, and alcohol habits, collected via Telegram bot intake.
  • Order & onboarding data: Name, WhatsApp number, email, and training goals.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous page views and feature usage collected only after cookie consent is granted.
  • Device & browser info: IP address and user agent via our hosting and CDN provider (Cloudflare).

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contract performance: To deliver coaching services, training programs, and tool access you requested.
  • Legitimate interest: For analytics, service improvement, and maintaining platform security.
  • Affirmative Consent: For processing special category health data (GDPR Art. 9), marketing communications, and tracking cookies/analytics.

4. Biometric Information Privacy Policy & FaceID Disclaimer

Muscle OS does NOT collect, capture, purchase, obtain, scan, or process biometric identifiers or biometric information (including retina or iris scans, fingerprints, voiceprints, or scans of hand or face geometry / FaceID) as defined under the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA 740 ILCS 14/), Texas CUBI, Washington RCW 19.375, and GDPR Article 9.

  • Athlete Photo Upload Policy: Athletes utilizing physique check-in tools are strictly instructed to crop, blur, or conceal their faces in all physique progress photos.
  • Zero Facial Recognition: Uploaded physique photos are stored locally in your browser sandbox or encrypted coach vault strictly for muscle silhouette progress comparison. No automated facial identification, geometric facial extraction, or biometric templates are ever generated.
  • Data Deletion: Any uploaded check-in photo can be deleted immediately by the athlete at any time.

5. Special Category Health Data (GDPR Art. 9)

We collect health-related data (including injury history, cardiac screening, and eating disorder screening) ONLY with your explicit affirmative consent. This data is used solely for the purpose of personalizing your training program and ensuring your physical safety. You can withdraw your consent at any time; however, this will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

6. Direct Marketing & Electronic Communications (Law 151/2020 & CAN-SPAM)

In strict compliance with Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 (Article 17) and the US CAN-SPAM Act, Muscle OS maintains a strict opt-in marketing policy:

  • We only send promotional emails, newsletters, and WhatsApp announcements to individuals who have provided affirmative, opt-in consent via an unticked checkbox or explicit form submission.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your contact information to third-party advertisers.
  • Every communication includes an immediate, free opt-out mechanism. You may unsubscribe at any time via our Unsubscribe Center or by replying "STOP" or "UNSUBSCRIBE" on WhatsApp or Telegram.
  • All testimonials and before/after transformation stories are published exclusively with written client consent and model release.

7. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

We use trusted third-party services to operate our platform, which may process your data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google OAuth: Authentication (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare Workers & KV: API, edge computing, and data storage (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase: Encrypted PostgreSQL database infrastructure (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Paymob & Vodafone Cash: Payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Groq & Google Gemini: Enterprise AI processing governed by zero-retention terms (user health data is never used to train public foundation models; see our AI Policy)
  • WhatsApp / Meta: Direct coaching communication (Meta Privacy Policy)

8. Cookies & Tracking Consent Gate

We respect your browsing privacy and deploy a strict consent gate:

  • Essential Storage: Used strictly for authentication, theme, and calculator calculations. These do not require consent under ePrivacy Directive exemptions.
  • Analytics & Marketing Trackers: Analytics tracking and any marketing pixels (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads) are permanently blocked from executing or injecting scripts until you grant explicit opt-in consent via our Cookie Banner.
  • You can review or revoke your consent preferences at any time by clicking Cookie Preferences in the footer.

9. Security & Data Encryption (GDPR Art. 32)

Muscle OS implements state-of-the-art cryptographic safeguards detailed in our Security Architecture:

  • In Transit: Mandatory TLS 1.3 encryption across all web traffic with 1-year HSTS enforcement.
  • At Rest: Cloud databases and KV stores are encrypted using AES-256 block ciphers.
  • Client-Side: Sensitive local intake drafts and health notes are obfuscated and salted via client-side cryptography.

10. Data Retention

  • Session tokens: 7 days
  • Trial access records: 30 days
  • Training/nutrition logs: Retained in browser sandbox until cleared or deletion requested
  • Cloud athlete profiles: Retained until account deletion request
  • Order & tax records: Retained for 5 years as required by Egyptian tax regulations
  • Analytics logs: 12 months maximum

11. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA/Law 151)

Depending on your location, you hold statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your profile and data.
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, portable JSON/CSV format.
  • Object / Opt-Out: Object to marketing or processing at any time via unsubscribe.html.
  • Withdraw Consent: Instantly withdraw consent for health screening data.
  • No Sale of Data: We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at https://wa.me/201040796017 or email privacy@muscleos.coach. We respond within 30 days.

12. Age Eligibility & Children's Privacy

Muscle OS services are intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 years or older (or individuals aged 16–17 who possess verifiable written parental or legal guardian consent). We strictly prohibit use by anyone under the age of 16 in compliance with COPPA and GDPR Article 8. If we discover that personal data of an individual under 16 has been collected without parental consent, we will immediately delete that data.
