03

8-12 Week Checkpoint

Assess every 8-12 weeks
▶ Continue Recomp

Body weight stable, waist decreasing, performance stable or increasing. The conditions for recomposition still exist. Stay the course and reassess in another 8-12 weeks.

▶ Switch to Dedicated Phase

No change in any metric for 4 consecutive weeks. Transition to a surplus (muscle gain) or deficit (fat loss) phase. The recomp window has closed — progress requires a targeted approach.
