This guide walks you through finding your calories, setting your macros, and building a sustainable eating structure. This and related topics are covered in depth in the Diet & Nutrition Book.

Step 2

Set Your Protein

Protein is your only non-negotiable macro. Set it by your goal and bodyweight.

Your GoalProtein (g per kg bodyweight)
Fat loss (preserve muscle)2.0-2.4 g/kg
Muscle gain1.6-2.2 g/kg
Maintenance / recomp1.6-2.0 g/kg

Example: 80 kg man in fat loss → 160-192 g protein per day.

Coach says: Eat protein at every meal. Spread it across 3-4 meals.
1 scoop of whey = ~25 g • 150 g chicken breast = ~45 g • Greek yogurt (200 g) = ~20 g
Step 3

Set Your Fat & Carbs

Set your fat minimum first. Carbs fill the rest.

Fat

ContextPer kgExample (80 kg)
Minimum0.5 g/kg40 g fat
Moderate0.8 g/kg64 g fat

Keep fat at minimum for fat loss phases. Go higher for maintenance or gain. Fat is non-negotiable for hormone health.

Carbs

Carbs fill the rest of your calories after protein and fat.

  • Training days: eat more carbs around your workout.
  • Rest days: eat fewer carbs, prioritise veggies.

Quick math: 1 g carb = 4 calories. If you have 800 kcal remaining after protein and fat, that's 200 g carbs.

Step 5

Hydration Basics

Water is the cheapest performance enhancer. Drink it like you mean it.

Activity LevelDaily Water Target
Sedentary2-2.5 L
Active (3-5 hrs/week)2.5-3.5 L
Very active (6-10 hrs/week)3.5-5 L

Urine check: Pale straw = hydrated. Dark amber like apple juice = drink more.

Training Day Protocol

  • 500 mL 2 hours before training
  • Sip 200-300 mL every 15 minutes during training
  • 500-1000 mL after training
Step 6

The 2-Week Check

Every 2 weeks, check your progress. This is your adjustment loop.

  1. Weigh yourself daily, track the weekly average.
  2. Are you losing/gaining at the expected rate?
    Fat loss: 0.5-1% body weight per week.
    Gain: 0.5-1.5% body weight per month.
  3. On track? Keep going. Don't change a thing.
  4. Not moving? Drop 200 kcal (or add 200 kcal for gain) and reassess in 2 weeks.
  5. Losing too fast (>1%/week)? Add 200 kcal.
Coach says: Water weight fluctuates 1-3 kg day to day. Don't panic. Watch the trend, not the daily number.

Common Mistakes

Avoid these. They will cost you weeks of progress.

  • Undertracking oils and condiments — that "healthy" salad with 3 tbsp of dressing just added 300 kcal.
  • Skipping protein at breakfast — then wondering why you're hungry at 11 AM.
  • Cutting calories too low too fast — you'll lose the first 5 kg then stall hard.
  • Ignoring the weekend — 4 days of deficit undone by 3 days of surplus still equals no progress.
  • Not adjusting after 2 weeks — the plan that worked at 80 kg won't work at 75 kg.

When You've Outgrown This Guide

This guide gets you 80% of the way there. If you're experiencing any of these, it's time to level up:

  • You've hit a plateau that won't budge after 2 adjustments
  • You're preparing for a competition or photoshoot
  • You have medical complexity (PCOS, thyroid, diabetes)
  • You want a fully customised plan with weekly adjustments
