What is a Training Split? A training split determines how you organise your workouts across the week — how often each muscle group is trained, how much volume per session, and how recovery is structured. Choosing the right split (full body, upper/lower, PPL, bro split, or powerlifting) is one of the most important programming decisions you can make.
